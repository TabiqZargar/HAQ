## Source policy

- An entry is eligible for retrieval only after its quoted text has been checked against the official source.
- Entries marked as model law or excerpt keep that label in the interface.
- The app does not invent section numbers, deadlines, or legal conclusions.
- If no verified passage is relevant, the app abstains and points to legal aid instead.
- Source links open only when the user clicks them. The app itself makes no external API calls.
